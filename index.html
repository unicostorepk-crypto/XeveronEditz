Everything you need to know about placing orders, turnarounds, revisions, and payment processing.

Our base price is $1.50 USD for a completed video of up to 10 seconds. For every additional 5 seconds beyond the initial 10 seconds, $0.50 USD is added. For example, a 30-second edit costs $3.50, and a 60-second edit costs $6.50.
Our target turnaround time is approximately 24 hours from the time footage and requirements are received. If your project involves complex motion tracking, custom visual effects, or extensive footage, delivery may require additional time and will be communicated upon order review.
